---
title: Using Picture in Picture
description: How to watch videos in a floating window while interacting with other sites, or applications.
---

Picture in Picture (PiP) allows you to watch videos in a floating window (always on top of other windows) so you can keep an eye on what you’re watching while interacting with other sites, or applications.
https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/picture-in-picture-extens/hkgfoiooedgoejojocmhlaklaeopbecg
