## Disable New Tab Ads
Open a new tab > Customize > Show sponsored backgrounds > Untoggle

## Disable Banners
Visit `brave://flags/` and search `brave-ads`. Then disable all the brave advertisement flags.

## Disable Rewards
Visit `brave://rewards/` and untoggle the "New tab page ads" and "Notification ads" options.
