---
title: How to Reinstall your Game Server
description: Welcome to the ForestRacks help center! Here, you will find comprehensive guides, tutorials, and reference materials to effectively utilize ForestRacks services.
---
<Stepper>
<StepperItem title="Login to Panel">
First login on [https://panel.forestracks.com](https://panel.forestracks.com) and click on your server. If you cannot login, follow the [Troubleshooting Guide](/games/troubleshoot/invalid-login).
</StepperItem>
<StepperItem title="Create a Backup">
Next click the "Backups" panel tab and click "Create Backup"
</StepperItem>
<StepperItem title="Delete Current Save">
Click the "File Manager" tab and delete everything
</StepperItem>
<StepperItem title="Run Reinstall">
Click the "Settings" tab and click the "Reinstall Server" button.
</StepperItem>
</Stepper>
