---
title: How do I contact support?
description: |
  ForestRacks support contact information: Discord, support tickets, Twitter, LinkedIn, and Instagram. Learn how to reach our team for sales inquiries, account issues, and technical support.
---
The quickest way to contact us is [Discord](https://discord.forestracks.com). However, this is reserved sales inquiries and general issues that are not associated with your account.

[Tickets](https://my.forestracks.com/submitticket) are another option, these are recommended for low urgency issues that may take a while for us to investigate.

The last option is our social media and forums; we have a Twitter, Linkedin, and Instagram. All of these will have slow response times but we will do our best to get back to you.
