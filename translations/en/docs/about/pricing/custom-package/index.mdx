### Value VPS Custom Pricing

Our Value VPS plans offer affordable virtualization for general-purpose workloads. Custom packages allow you to tailor resources to your specific needs.

| Resource | Price per Unit |
|----------|----------------|
| vCPU Cores | $1.00/core/month |
| RAM | $0.75/GB/month |
| Storage (SSD) | $0.10/GB/month |
| Bandwidth | $0.50/TB/month |
| IPv4 Address | $3.00/additional IP/month |
| IPv6 Address | /64 included |

**Example Custom Configuration:**
* 4 vCPU Cores: 4 × $1.00 = $4.00
* 8GB RAM: 8 × $0.75 = $6.00
* 100GB SSD: 100 × $0.10 = $10.00
* 5TB Bandwidth: 5 × $0.50 = $2.50
* 1 IPv4 (included): $0.00
* **Total: $22.50/month**

<Note>
Custom VPS configurations require a minimum commitment of 3 months. Contact our sales team for volume discounts on orders of 5+ servers.
</Note>
