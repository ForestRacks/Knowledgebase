## 9. Cookies and Similar Technologies:
We use cookies and similar technologies on our website to improve the user experience and to better understand our customers' needs. Cookies are small text files that are stored on a user's device when they visit a website.

We use the following types of cookies on our website:

Essential cookies: These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly and cannot be turned off. They are typically only set in response to actions made by you, such as logging in or filling out forms.
Performance cookies: These cookies collect information about how users interact with our website, such as which pages are visited most often and whether users receive error messages. We use this information to improve the performance of our website.
Functional cookies: These cookies are used to remember user preferences, such as language preference, and to provide a more personalized user experience.
Advertising cookies: These cookies are used to track user behavior and to deliver targeted advertising.
We rely on your consent for the use of non-essential cookies and similar technologies on our website. By continuing to use our website, you consent to our use of these technologies. You can manage your cookie preferences by clicking on the cookie settings link on our website.

Please be aware that if you choose to disable or delete cookies on your device, it may negatively impact the overall functionality of our website.
