## 14. Data Retention:
ForestRacks retains personal data only for as long as necessary to: (i) fulfill the purposes for which it was collected as outlined in this Policy; (ii) comply with applicable legal, tax, and accounting obligations, including retention periods mandated by law (typically seven (7) years for financial records); (iii) resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, and protect our legal rights; and (iv) support legitimate business purposes.

### Specific Retention Periods:
(i) **Active Account Data:** Retained for the duration of your account plus thirty (30) days after account closure;
(ii) **Deleted Content and Account Deletion:** When you delete your account or specific content (such as projects, billing sites, or files), we implement a soft delete process: data is immediately removed from your account view and locked for thirty (30) days. During this recovery period, the data is inaccessible but recoverable in case of accidental deletion. After thirty (30) days, data is permanently deleted from all primary systems and backups, unless legal retention is required;
(iii) **Billing Records:** Seven (7) years from transaction date for tax and accounting compliance, including payment processor logs and transaction histories;
(iv) **System Backups:** Backup systems may retain encrypted copies of data for up to ninety (90) days after deletion from primary systems, after which they are permanently purged;
(v) **Server Logs:** Ninety (90) days for operational data, thirty (30) days for API logs;
(vi) **Support Tickets:** Two (2) years from resolution date;
(vii) **Marketing Communications:** Until consent withdrawal plus suppression list retention;
(viii) **Enterprise Data:** Subject to custom retention periods as specified in enterprise agreements;
(ix) **Legal Holds:** Indefinitely when subject to litigation hold or regulatory investigation;
(x) **Network Traffic Logs:** DNS query logs are retained for one (1) day; HTTP access logs for seven (7) days; DDoS mitigation logs for thirty (30) days; security incident logs for ninety (90) days; and firewall logs for fourteen (14) days. These logs may contain IP addresses, timestamps, and request information necessary for security and troubleshooting purposes;
(xi) **Email Logs:** SMTP transaction logs retained for thirty (30) days; email rate limiting logs for seven (7) days; spam detection logs for fourteen (14) days. These logs are maintained for anti-abuse purposes and may be extended if investigating policy violations;
(xii) **Performance Monitoring Data:** Resource usage metrics retained for thirty (30) days; bandwidth usage statistics for ninety (90) days; uptime monitoring data for one (1) year. Aggregated performance data may be retained indefinitely in anonymized form.

### Zero Data Retention Options:
For certain services, ForestRacks may offer zero data retention options where technically feasible. When enabled: (i) no logs are created for the specified activities; (ii) data is processed only in volatile memory; and (iii) no persistent storage occurs. This option may affect service functionality and support capabilities.

Upon expiration of the applicable retention period, personal data will be securely deleted using industry-standard data sanitization methods or irreversibly anonymized. You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to our legal retention obligations.
