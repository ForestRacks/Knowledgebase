## 1. Introduction:

Forest Racks LLC and its affiliates (collectively "ForestRacks", "we", "us" or "our") respect the privacy of our customers, users, and visitors ("you", "your", "subscriber", "subscribers", "customer", "customers", "client", or "clients") and are committed to protecting your personal information. ### Transparency Commitment

Personal data is any information relating to an identified or identifiable living person. When collecting and using personal data, our policy is to be transparent about why and how we process personal data. The personal data provided to us comes either directly from the individual concerned, from third parties acting on behalf of an individual, or from publicly available sources. Where we receive personal data about an individual from third parties, we request that the third party inform the individual of the necessary information regarding the use of their data.

The type of information we collect depends on how you use our Services. Generally, we collect the following categories:

(i) **Identifiers and Contact Information:** Name, username, account ID, postal address, email address, phone number, unique device identifiers, mobile device ID, advertising ID (IDFA, Android ID), IP address, and similar identifiers;

## 4. Storage and Protection:

### Security Measures

We take the security of all data we hold seriously. Our security framework includes:

(i) **Technical Safeguards**: Encryption of data in transit and at rest, secure servers, firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and regular security audits;

(ii) **Organizational Measures**: Staff training on data protection, confidentiality agreements, access controls, and regular policy reviews;

(iii) **Physical Security**: Secured data center facilities with restricted access, surveillance systems, and environmental controls;

(iv) **Incident Response**: Established procedures for detecting, reporting, and responding to data security incidents;

(v) **Vulnerability Management**: Regular security assessments, penetration testing, and prompt patching of identified vulnerabilities.

We store your data in secure servers and take necessary steps to prevent unauthorized access or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security. Any transmission of personal data is at your own risk.

## 14. Data Retention:
ForestRacks retains personal data only for as long as necessary to: (i) fulfill the purposes for which it was collected as outlined in this Policy; (ii) comply with applicable legal, tax, and accounting obligations, including retention periods mandated by law (typically seven (7) years for financial records); (iii) resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, and protect our legal rights; and (iv) support legitimate business purposes.

### Specific Retention Periods:
(i) **Active Account Data:** Retained for the duration of your account plus thirty (30) days after account closure;
(ii) **Deleted Content and Account Deletion:** When you delete your account or specific content (such as projects, billing sites, or files), we implement a soft delete process: data is immediately removed from your account view and locked for thirty (30) days. During this recovery period, the data is inaccessible but recoverable in case of accidental deletion. After thirty (30) days, data is permanently deleted from all primary systems and backups, unless legal retention is required;
(iii) **Billing Records:** Seven (7) years from transaction date for tax and accounting compliance, including payment processor logs and transaction histories;
(iv) **System Backups:** Backup systems may retain encrypted copies of data for up to ninety (90) days after deletion from primary systems, after which they are permanently purged;
(v) **Server Logs:** Ninety (90) days for operational data, thirty (30) days for API logs;
(vi) **Support Tickets:** Two (2) years from resolution date;
(vii) **Marketing Communications:** Until consent withdrawal plus suppression list retention;
(viii) **Enterprise Data:** Subject to custom retention periods as specified in enterprise agreements;
(ix) **Legal Holds:** Indefinitely when subject to litigation hold or regulatory investigation;
(x) **Network Traffic Logs:** DNS query logs are retained for one (1) day; HTTP access logs for seven (7) days; DDoS mitigation logs for thirty (30) days; security incident logs for ninety (90) days; and firewall logs for fourteen (14) days. These logs may contain IP addresses, timestamps, and request information necessary for security and troubleshooting purposes;
(xi) **Email Logs:** SMTP transaction logs retained for thirty (30) days; email rate limiting logs for seven (7) days; spam detection logs for fourteen (14) days. These logs are maintained for anti-abuse purposes and may be extended if investigating policy violations;
(xii) **Performance Monitoring Data:** Resource usage metrics retained for thirty (30) days; bandwidth usage statistics for ninety (90) days; uptime monitoring data for one (1) year. Aggregated performance data may be retained indefinitely in anonymized form.

### Zero Data Retention Options:
For certain services, ForestRacks may offer zero data retention options where technically feasible. When enabled: (i) no logs are created for the specified activities; (ii) data is processed only in volatile memory; and (iii) no persistent storage occurs. This option may affect service functionality and support capabilities.

Upon expiration of the applicable retention period, personal data will be securely deleted using industry-standard data sanitization methods or irreversibly anonymized. You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to our legal retention obligations.

## 19. Law Enforcement and Government Data Requests:

ForestRacks shall respond to lawful requests from law enforcement agencies and governmental authorities in accordance with applicable laws and legal process requirements. The scope of ForestRacks' disclosure obligations shall be determined by the nature of the legal process received and the jurisdiction from which it originates.

### 19.1 United States Legal Process
For requests originating from United States law enforcement or governmental agencies, ForestRacks shall disclose information in accordance with the following framework: (a) in response to administrative or grand jury subpoenas issued pursuant to statutory authority, ForestRacks may disclose basic subscriber information including but not limited to name, email address, IP address, and payment method information; (b) in response to court orders issued by courts of competent jurisdiction, ForestRacks may disclose additional account information and transactional data as specified in such orders; (c) in response to search warrants issued upon probable cause, ForestRacks may disclose content data and comprehensive account information; (d) in response to National Security Letters issued pursuant to applicable federal statutes, ForestRacks shall disclose information to the extent required by law; and (e) in exigent circumstances involving imminent risk of death or serious physical injury, ForestRacks may disclose information necessary to address such emergency upon receipt of appropriate emergency disclosure requests from law enforcement.

### 19.2 International Legal Process
Legal process originating from jurisdictions outside the United States shall be processed as follows: (a) requests from foreign governments must be submitted through applicable Mutual Legal Assistance Treaties (MLATs), letters rogatory, or other established diplomatic channels; (b) direct foreign requests shall be honored only where ForestRacks is legally compelled to comply under the laws of the jurisdiction in which ForestRacks operates or where data is stored; (c) requests from United Kingdom or European Union authorities may be processed pursuant to applicable data sharing agreements and frameworks; and (d) ForestRacks shall provide notice to affected users of legal process received unless prohibited by law or court order from doing so.

### 19.3 Transparency Reporting
ForestRacks shall publish transparency reports on a semi-annual basis detailing the aggregate number and categories of government data requests received, to the extent permitted by applicable law and not prohibited by court order or other legal restrictions.
