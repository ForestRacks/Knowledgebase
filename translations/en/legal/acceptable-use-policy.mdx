## 1. Introduction:
This Acceptable Use Policy (the "Policy") is a legally binding agreement between Forest Racks, LLC d/b/a ForestRacks and its affiliates (collectively "ForestRacks") and the subscriber (the "Subscriber" or "you") that governs the use of ForestRacks' services (the "Services") including hosting services, LIR services, colocation, SaaS platforms, and domain registration. This Policy applies globally to all customers regardless of location and is designed to ensure fair use, network stability, legal compliance, and a safe environment for all users.

## 2. Prohibited Activities:
The Subscriber shall not use the Services for any activities that are illegal, malicious, unethical, or that abuse our network:

(i) Legal Violations: Violating any applicable laws or regulations in any jurisdiction; facilitating illegal activities or criminal enterprises; money laundering or terrorist financing; export control or sanctions violations; activities involving chemical, biological, or nuclear weapons, nuclear explosives, or missiles capable of delivering such weapons without proper export authorization; violation of the International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR) or Export Administration Regulations (EAR); or any other illegal activity.

(ii) Network and Security Abuse: Denial of Service (DoS/DDoS) attacks; botting; port scanning or vulnerability scanning without authorization; attempting to breach security systems or authentication measures; distributing malware, viruses, trojans, ransomware, or other malicious code; operating open proxies, open mail relays, or TOR exit nodes without explicit written permission; cryptocurrency mining without explicit written permission; running public DNS resolvers without permission; announcing IP addresses not allocated to Subscriber; configuring PTR records for domains not owned by Subscriber; BGP hijacking or route manipulation; DNS spoofing or cache poisoning; hacking, cracking, and nulling activities; proxying or tunneling to another provider without obtaining prior written permission from ForestRacks; or any form of network resource theft or fraud.

(iii) Content Violations: Copyright infringement or piracy; trademark violations; distribution of illegal content including child sexual abuse material (CSAM) which will be immediately reported to NCMEC and law enforcement; harassment, stalking, or threats; hate speech or discrimination based on protected characteristics; terrorist content or violent extremism; or any content that violates applicable law.

(iv) Spam and Messaging Abuse: Spam, phishing, or unsolicited bulk email; SMS/text message spam; unsolicited commercial communications; sending marketing emails without prior written permission from ForestRacks; email bombing; or operating open mail relays without permission.

(v) Fraudulent and Deceptive Practices: Pyramid schemes or multi-level marketing schemes without proper disclosure; "Make-Money Fast" schemes; ponzi schemes; fraudulent business practices; sale of counterfeit goods; deceptive marketing practices; impersonation or misrepresentation; promoting fake health products and remedies; offering stock from unknown startups; participating in chain letter schemes; or financial fraud of any kind.

(vi) Controlled Substances and Regulated Products: Unlicensed pharmaceutical sales; unauthorized distribution of controlled substances; sale of illegal drugs or drug paraphernalia; promoting, encouraging, or engaging in the sale or distribution of prescription medication without a valid prescription; or violation of FDA, DEA, or similar regulatory requirements.

(i) IP Address and Network Services: Subscriber must comply with all applicable Regional Internet Registry policies; maintain accurate WHOIS information; provide proper justification for IP allocations; refrain from IP squatting or hoarding; and is strictly prohibited from network routing manipulation or IP hijacking.

(ii) Colocation Services: All equipment must meet facility safety standards; no unauthorized modifications to facility infrastructure are permitted; compliance with all applicable fire and electrical codes is required; and all equipment must be properly labeled and documented.

(iii) Software as a Service: Reverse engineering or decompiling is prohibited; API rate limits must be respected; payment card industry (PCI-DSS) compliance is required for payment processing functions; and all data processing must comply with applicable privacy laws.

(iv) Domain Services: Accurate registrant information must be maintained; domain squatting or typosquatting is prohibited; trademark infringement is strictly forbidden; and compliance with all registry and registrar policies is required.

(v) Age-Restricted Content: Adult content is permitted only on designated servers with prior written approval and must comply with 18 U.S.C. § 2257 record-keeping requirements where applicable.

(vi) Regulated Commercial Activities: Online gambling, betting, or gaming services require proof of appropriate licensing in all jurisdictions where services are offered and must implement age verification systems. Cannabis-related businesses must operate only where legal and comply with all applicable regulations.

## 8. Fair Use and Resource Allocation:

(i) Shared Resources: On shared services, excessive resource consumption that adversely affects other customers is prohibited, including but not limited to: CPU usage consistently exceeding twenty-five percent (25%) on shared hosting; memory usage exceeding allocated limits; disk I/O operations that impact server performance; or database queries exceeding fifteen (15) seconds.

(ii) Network Fair Use: Bandwidth usage must be primarily for serving content to end users; services shall not be used as CDN origin without appropriate CDN plan; and outbound email volume shall be limited to prevent spam and maintain deliverability.

(iii) Network Filters and Rate Limiting: ForestRacks implements various network filters and rate limiting policies to protect infrastructure and ensure service quality for all customers. This includes but is not limited to: (a) email sending rate limits to prevent spam; (b) DDoS protection filters that may block or challenge suspicious traffic; (c) connection rate limits to prevent abuse; (d) API request rate limiting; (e) DNS query rate limiting; and (f) other automated security measures. ForestRacks reserves the right to rate limit or block emails at any time for any reason at its sole discretion. These filters may occasionally affect legitimate traffic, and ForestRacks is not liable for any impact on services resulting from these protective measures.

(iv) Bandwidth Management: While services may be advertised as "unmetered," this means Subscriber is not charged based on bandwidth usage under normal conditions. However: (a) bandwidth may be throttled from 1Gbps to 100Mbps after reaching 10TB of monthly usage; (b) excessive bandwidth usage that impacts other customers may result in immediate throttling or suspension; (c) sustained usage exceeding 25% of system resources for more than 90 seconds may trigger automatic throttling; and (d) ForestRacks reserves the right to implement fair use policies at its discretion.

(v) High Risk Use Prohibition: ForestRacks Services are neither designed nor intended for use in situations where their failure could lead to death or serious bodily injury of any person, or to severe physical or environmental damage ("High Risk Use"). You are not permitted to use the Services in, or in connection with, High Risk Use. High Risk Use includes, for example: aircraft or other modes of human mass transportation, nuclear or chemical facilities, life support systems, implantable medical devices, motor vehicles, or any other application where product failure could lead to loss of life or catastrophic property damage. You shall indemnify and hold ForestRacks harmless against any third-party claim arising out of your breach of this provision.
