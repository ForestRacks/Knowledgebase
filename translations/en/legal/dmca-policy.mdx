##  1. Introduction

At ForestRacks, we take copyright infringement seriously. If you believe that copyrighted content is being hosted on our servers, we urge you to submit a proper DMCA request in accordance with 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3)(A).

Submit DMCA Reports to [abuse+dmca@forestracks.com](mailto:abuse+dmca@forestracks.com) When filing a complaint, please ensure that you are the copyright holder or an authorized representative. Include proof of valid copyright ownership with your submission.

<Note type="note" title="Important Notice">
  For the most efficient resolution, we recommend providing a court-ordered DMCA takedown request whenever possible. This helps ensure prompt and accurate processing of your claim.
</Note>
