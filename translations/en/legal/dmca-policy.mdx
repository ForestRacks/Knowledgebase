Forest Racks LLC ("ForestRacks," "we," "us," or "our") respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects our users to do the same. In accordance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act of 1998 ("DMCA"), specifically 17 U.S.C. § 512, we will respond expeditiously to claims of copyright infringement committed using our services that are reported to our designated DMCA Agent.

This policy outlines our procedures for receiving and responding to notices of alleged copyright infringement, as well as the process for submitting counter-notifications. We reserve the right to remove content alleged to be infringing without prior notice, at our sole discretion, and without liability to you.

<Note type="note" title="Important Notice">

Under Section 512(f) of the DMCA, any person who knowingly materially misrepresents that material or activity is infringing may be subject to liability for damages. Consult with an attorney before filing a notice if you are unsure whether your rights are being infringed.

## 3. Response Timeline

Upon receipt of a valid DMCA notice, ForestRacks will:

(i) **Acknowledge Receipt**: Send confirmation within one (1) business day of receiving the notice;

(ii) **Initial Review**: Conduct preliminary review within twenty-four (24) hours to determine if the notice contains all required elements;

(iii) **Action Timeline**: Take appropriate action within seventy-two (72) hours of receiving a complete and valid notice, which may include:
   - Removing or disabling access to the allegedly infringing material
   - Notifying the alleged infringer of the takedown
   - Providing the alleged infringer with a copy of the takedown notice

(iv) **Documentation**: Maintain records of all notices and actions taken for at least three (3) years.

## 4. Filing a DMCA Takedown Notice

To file a notice of infringement with us, you must provide a written communication that sets forth the items specified below. Please note that you will be liable for damages (including costs and attorneys' fees) if you materially misrepresent that material is infringing.

### 4.1 Required Information for Valid DMCA Notice

Your DMCA takedown notice must include ALL of the following information:

1. **Physical or Electronic Signature**: A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed. Electronic signatures may include typed names preceded by "/s/" or established digital signature methods.

2. **Identification of Copyrighted Work**: Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed, or, if multiple copyrighted works at a single online site are covered by a single notification, a representative list of such works at that site.

3. **Identification of Infringing Material**: Identification of the material that is claimed to be infringing or to be the subject of infringing activity and that is to be removed or access to which is to be disabled, and information reasonably sufficient to permit us to locate the material. **Please provide specific URLs or IP addresses where the alleged infringing content can be found.**

4. **Contact Information**: Information reasonably sufficient to permit us to contact you, such as an address, telephone number, and, if available, an email address at which you may be contacted.

5. **Good Faith Statement**: A statement that you have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.

6. **Accuracy and Authority Statement**: A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.

## 6. Counter-Notification Procedures

If you believe that material you posted was wrongfully removed or disabled, you may file a counter-notification with us. The counter-notification must include:

1. **Your Physical or Electronic Signature**

2. **Identification of Removed Material**: Identification of the material that has been removed or to which access has been disabled and the location at which the material appeared before it was removed or access to it was disabled.

3. **Statement Under Penalty of Perjury**: A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification of the material to be removed or disabled.

4. **Contact Information**: Your name, address, and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the Federal District Court for the judicial district in which your address is located (or the Federal District Court for the Northern District of California if your address is outside of the United States), and that you will accept service of process from the person who provided the original DMCA notification or an agent of such person.

Upon receipt of a valid counter-notification, we will:

(i) **Review Period**: Review the counter-notification within two (2) business days for completeness;

(ii) **Forward to Complainant**: Promptly provide the original complainant with a copy of the counter-notification;

(iii) **Waiting Period**: Wait ten (10) to fourteen (14) business days before restoring content;

(iv) **Restoration Notice**: Inform both parties when content is restored;

(v) **Court Action Exception**: Not restore content if we receive notice of court action from the original complainant.

## 7. Repeat Infringer Policy

In accordance with the DMCA and other applicable laws, ForestRacks has adopted a policy of terminating, in appropriate circumstances and at our sole discretion, the accounts of users who are deemed to be repeat infringers. We may also, at our sole discretion, limit access to our services and/or terminate the accounts of any users who infringe any intellectual property rights of others, whether or not there is any repeat infringement.

### 7.1 Repeat Infringer Criteria

A user may be considered a repeat infringer if:
- They receive three (3) or more valid DMCA takedown notices within a twelve (12) month period
- They repeatedly upload content that has been previously removed for copyright infringement
- They demonstrate a pattern of copyright-infringing behavior
- They attempt to circumvent content identification systems
- They reupload identical or substantially similar infringing content after removal

### 7.2 Strike System

(i) **First Strike**: Warning notice and content removal;

(ii) **Second Strike**: Temporary suspension of upload privileges for seven (7) days;

(iii) **Third Strike**: Account termination and permanent ban from services;

(iv) **Appeal Process**: Users may appeal strikes through our support system within thirty (30) days.

## 9. Preservation of Content

When we receive a valid DMCA takedown notice, we may preserve a copy of the allegedly infringing content for our records and potential legal proceedings. This preserved content:

(i) **Access Restrictions**: Will not be publicly accessible and will be stored in a segregated environment;

(ii) **Limited Disclosure**: May be shared only with:
   - Legal counsel representing ForestRacks
   - Law enforcement pursuant to valid legal process
   - Courts or arbitrators in connection with disputes
   - The copyright owner or their authorized representatives for verification purposes

(iii) **Retention Period**: Will be retained for the longer of:
   - Three (3) years from the date of the notice
   - The duration of any related legal proceedings plus one (1) year
   - As required by applicable law or court order

(iv) **Security Measures**: Will be encrypted and access-logged to maintain chain of custody.
